Each knowledge base works like a shared drive: organize files into folders, find what you need with search and filters, preview files, and choose who can access the base.

## Inside a knowledge base

The top of a base shows its name, who created it, and its access — **Private**, **Shared base**, or your organization's name for an organization-wide base. On the right:

- **Chat** starts a new chat scoped to everything in the base.
- **Share** opens the share dialog. See [Sharing a knowledge base](#sharing-a-knowledge-base).
- The **⋮** menu has **Rename** and **Delete** for the base, if you can change it.

Click **Back to hub** to return to the list of bases. Breadcrumbs above the file list show which folder you're in; click any breadcrumb to go back up.

The file list shows each item's **Name**, who added it, its type, and the **Date added**. Click a column header to sort. By default, folders come first, newest files are on top, and files that are still processing stay at the top until they finish.

## Folders

Click the **Create folder** icon in the toolbar, enter a name, and click **Create**. To group existing files, select them and click **New folder with selected** in the selection toolbar.

Folders can be nested up to **10 levels** deep, counting the knowledge base itself. Folder names can't be empty, start with a dot, or contain `/ \ : * ? " < > |`.

## Find files

- **Type** — the dropdown at the left of the toolbar narrows the list to **Folders**, **Documents**, **Images**, **Spreadsheets**, or **Slides**. Choose **All files** to reset it.
- **Search** — click the magnifier icon and type part of a file name.

<Note>
Advanced filters are available on select plans. With them, the toolbar also lets you filter by **Category**, **Format**, **Provider**, **Added by**, **Coverage**, **Document date**, and **Added** date, and the file list shows **Category** and **Provider** columns. Merciv reads these details from each file when it's processed.
</Note>

## Preview a file

Click a file to open a full preview. Use **Download** to save the original, **Fullscreen** to expand the preview, and **Close** to return to the list.

The preview has its own link: copy the page URL while a preview is open and send it to a teammate to take them straight to that file. They still need access to the knowledge base to see it.

<Note>
On plans with advanced filters, the preview also shows a details panel with the file's **Summary**, who added it, its processing **Status**, the date it was added, its size, and extracted details such as **Category**, **Provider**, **Document date**, and **Coverage**.
</Note>

## Work with selected files

Check one or more rows to open a toolbar at the bottom of the page:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chat with selected** | Starts a new chat scoped to the selected files and folders |
| **Download** | Downloads the selected files |
| **New folder with selected** | Creates a folder and moves the selection into it |
| **Rename** | Renames the item (one item at a time) |
| **Delete** | Deletes the selection |
| **Move to…** | Moves the selection to another folder or knowledge base |

Download and Chat with selected work for anyone who can see the files. The other actions need permission to change every selected item, and Rename, Move, and New folder are unavailable while a selected file is still processing.

You can also move files by dragging rows onto a folder in the list.

## Move and rename knowledge bases

From the Knowledge page, hover over a base's card and click **⋯** for **Rename**, **Move to…**, and **Delete**. Moving a base into another base turns it into a folder inside that base.

## Sharing a knowledge base

Access is set for the whole knowledge base — files and folders inside it follow the base. Click **Share** in the base's header to open the share dialog.

- **Invite people.** Click **Add emails to invite people**, choose teammates, choose **View only** (viewer) or **Full access** (editor), and click **Invite**.
- **Change or remove access.** In the list of people, change someone between **View only** and **Full access**, or choose **Remove access**. The base's creator is shown as **Owner**.
- **Share with your organization.** Under **General access**, switch between **Only people invited** and **Anyone in the organization**. Making a base organization-wide replaces individual invitations with access for everyone. Switching back to **Only people invited** removes organization access, and you can then invite people individually.
- **Copy link** copies a link to the base. People without access can't open it.

To change sharing, you need to be an organization admin who either created the base or has **Full access** to it. Everyone else can open the dialog to see who has access, but can't change it.

## Delete files, folders, and bases

Select items and click **Delete**, or use **Delete** in a base's **⋮** menu. Merciv lists what will be deleted and asks you to confirm. Deleting a folder or base also deletes everything inside it.

<Warning>
Deleting can't be undone from the app. If you deleted something by mistake, email [hello@merciv.com](mailto:hello@merciv.com) as soon as possible.
</Warning>

Who can delete: the base's creator and people with **Full access**. In **Organization** bases, organization admins.
