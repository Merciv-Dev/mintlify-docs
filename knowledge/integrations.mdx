Integrations connect Merciv to the tools your team already works in — Slack, Notion, Gmail, Google Drive, HubSpot, and many more. Once an app is connected, the assistant can read from it and take actions in it on your behalf during a chat.

<Note>
Integrations are available on select plans. If you don't see **Integrations** in your account menu, contact your Merciv representative.
</Note>

## Open Integrations

Click your name at the bottom of the left navigation to open the account menu, then click **Integrations**.

The page has three tabs:

- **Tools** — apps the assistant can use in chat. Apps you've connected appear first under **Connected**, followed by **Popular** apps and the rest of the catalog grouped by category.
- **Files** — cloud storage sources an organization admin can sync into Knowledge. See [Sync files into Knowledge](#sync-files-into-knowledge).
- **Data** — reserved for structured data sources. Nothing is available to connect here yet.

Use the search box to find an app by name, and on the **Tools** tab, the category filter to browse one category at a time.

## Connect an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the app">
    On the **Tools** tab, click the app's card or its **+** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and connect">
    Merciv explains what the connection allows. Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve access">
    Sign in to the app if asked and approve access. You're returned to Integrations, and the app moves to **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your permissions carry over: the assistant can only reach what your own account in that app can already open. Connections you make here are personal — they only act as you.

Your plan sets how many apps each person can connect. If you reach the limit, Merciv shows a dialog where you can request more.

## Use connected apps in chat

The assistant can use your connected apps whenever they help answer a question. To point it at a specific app, click **Integrations** below the chat composer and pick the app, or type **@** in the composer and choose it under **Integrations**. See [Sources and attachments](/chat/sources-and-attachments).

## Manage or disconnect an app

Click a connected app's card to see when it was connected, whether it's **Personal** or **Shared**, and when it was last used. From there:

- **Reconnect** if the app reports a problem or its sign-in has expired.
- **Disconnect** to remove the connection. Disconnecting a **Personal** connection only affects you; disconnecting a **Shared** connection affects everyone in your organization. Anything already copied into Knowledge stays there.

## Connect a custom tool

If your team runs its own MCP server — for example, an internal search or data service — you can connect it as a custom tool.

<Note>
Custom tools are available on select plans.
</Note>

1. On the **Tools** tab, click **New custom**.
2. Enter a **Name** and the **Server URL** (it must use HTTPS).
3. Under **Authentication**, choose **None**, **API key**, or **OAuth 2.0**, and fill in the details your server needs.
4. Under **Availability**, choose **Just me** or **Organization**. Only organization admins can make a custom tool available to the organization.
5. Click **Connect**.

Custom tools appear under **Connected** with the name you gave them, and the assistant sees that name too. For a custom tool available to the organization, organization admins can open its card and choose who can use it: **Everyone in the organization** or **Restricted to specific people**. Click **Save access** to apply the change.

## Sync files into Knowledge

Organization admins can connect a cloud storage source on the **Files** tab — Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint, or Amazon S3 — and Merciv copies its files into Knowledge so the assistant can find and cite them. Syncing can pull in a large library, so it's limited to organization admins.

<Note>
File sync is available on select plans and requires [Knowledge](/knowledge/overview).
</Note>

Permissions from the source carry over: each person can only find synced files that their own account in that source can open.

Click a connected source to see its sync status and when it last synced. From there you can:

- **Edit** the sync scope to limit it to specific folders (or sites and libraries), or leave it empty to sync everything the linked account can see. Click **Save scope** to apply.
- **Re-sync** to pull changes now.
- **Reconnect** if the status shows **Sync has stalled** or **Sync stopped**.

Merciv checks for changes on a regular schedule, so new and updated files arrive without any action from you.
