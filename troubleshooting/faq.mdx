## Privacy and access

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is my data private?">
    Yes, by default. Everything you create — chats, reports, projects, and private knowledge bases — is private to you until you share it. Files in an **Organization** knowledge base are visible to your whole organization; files in a **Private** base are visible only to you and anyone you share the base with. All content is scoped to your organization: people outside it can't see your work unless you create a public link. See [Sharing and permissions](/getting-started/sharing-and-permissions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see my chats?">
    Only you. Chats are private to their creator and have no share option. To share what a chat produced, share the generated [artifact](/artifacts/overview) or put the content in a [report](/reports/overview), both of which support sharing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I share a chat with a teammate?">
    No — chats can't be shared. Share the artifact the chat generated, or create a report from the content. Projects, reports, artifacts, and stories all support per-user sharing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can people outside my organization view a report?">
    Yes, through a public link. Anyone with the link gets read-only access — public visitors can never edit. Switching off **Anyone with the link** invalidates the link immediately; switching it back on creates a new one. See [Sharing and exporting](/reports/sharing-and-exporting).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I share an item I can view?">
    Only editors and owners can share. If you see "You do not have permission to share this item," ask the owner to change your access to **Full access**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Files and Knowledge

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What file types are supported?">
    [Knowledge](/knowledge/overview) accepts PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, Parquet, SPSS (`.sav`), Markdown, and images (jpg, jpeg, png, webp), up to 100 MB per file and 2,500 files or 25 GB per upload. Chat attachments follow their own limits: 5 files per message, 25 MB per file, 100 MB combined — and chat also accepts plain text, HTML, XML, and JSON. See [Uploading files](/knowledge/uploading-files).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does file processing take?">
    Processing happens in the background and typically takes a few minutes. Large or scanned documents take longer because every page is processed individually. A file isn't usable by chat or search until processing finishes. If processing can't complete, the file shows **Failed** — upload it again, and contact support if it fails a second time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I upload to a knowledge base?">
    To upload into a knowledge base you need to be able to change it: you created it, you have full access to it, or it's an **Organization** base and you're an organization admin. Create your own base, or ask the base's owner for access. See [Uploading files](/knowledge/uploading-files).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I shared a document — why can't my teammate's chat find it yet?">
    Sharing changes take a moment to reach chat and search. A short lag is normal; if a shared file stays invisible to a teammate well beyond a few minutes, contact support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I delete something?">
    Deleting a chat, report, project, gallery, or file archives it: it disappears from your lists and its link stops working. There's no self-service restore, so delete deliberately. Archived trackers are the exception with a visible Archived tab — and they're permanently deleted after 30 days.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Chat, trackers, and stories

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How accurate are Merciv's answers?">
    Merciv is AI and can make mistakes. Answers include citations for a reason — double-check cited sources before acting on a finding, especially for high-stakes decisions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my new chat unnamed?">
    Chat names are generated in the background after your first message, so a short delay is normal. A chat that never gets a name still works fine.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often can a tracker run?">
    Manual, Daily, Weekly (the default), or Monthly. Scheduled runs fire on the hour or half hour in the tracker's timezone; Manual trackers only run when you click **Run Now**. See [Managing trackers](/trackers/managing-trackers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get more trackers?">
    Tracker count is set by your organization's plan. When you hit the limit, you'll see "You're tracking as much as this plan allows." Archive trackers you no longer need, or contact your Merciv representative about a higher limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='Why did my tracker run show "Skipped"?'>
    Nothing new turned up for that period, or every candidate topic overlapped with stories the tracker already published. Skipped is deduplication working as intended, not a failure. Broaden the topic, add sources, or lower the frequency if it happens often.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long until a story appears after a run starts?">
    Story generation runs in the background and can take several minutes; the tracker page keeps checking for up to 30 minutes. The finished story appears in your [Discover feed](/discover/overview) and on the tracker's run history.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account and workspace

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I sign up with a personal email?">
    No. Merciv is available for work email addresses; personal domains are blocked at signup. See [Getting access](/account/getting-access).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I invite teammates?">
    Organization admins and owners invite by email from **Settings → Members** or the account menu → **Invite**, choosing **Collab**, **Full Access**, or **Org Admin** access. Admins can also approve up to 3 email domains so anyone signing up with a matching work email joins automatically as a member. Depending on your plan, anyone can also share a personal invite link. See [Managing your organization](/admin/managing-your-organization).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see a feature my colleague has?">
    Some features — Playbooks, Portfolio, Galleries, Integrations, and certain chat options among them — are available on select plans, and availability is set per organization. Your role matters too: organization settings and member management are only for admins and owners. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see a feature you expect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't search find my artifact or uploaded file?">
    Search (Cmd/Ctrl+K) matches names, not the text inside documents. With the **All** filter it covers chats, reports, artifacts, projects, galleries where available, stories, and Knowledge files and folders you can access. If a name search doesn't find it, click **Find with Agent** to have Merciv look for it in a new chat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Merciv work on mobile?">
    The web app adapts to small screens: the sidebar becomes an overlay and chat opens full-screen rather than in a side panel. On a phone, pressing Enter in the chat box adds a new line — tap the send button to send.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see my plan or credits?">
    Plans are set up with Merciv rather than managed in the app, so there's no billing page. When your organization reaches a limit — including running out of credits — Merciv shows which limit you hit, and organization admins can click **Request Access** to talk to the Merciv team. See [Billing and plans](/admin/billing-and-plans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Merciv tell me when a long answer is ready?">
    Yes. Turn on **Chat reply notifications** in **Settings → Notifications**, or click **Notify me** on the hint above the chat box while an answer is running. You'll get a notice when Merciv replies in a chat you're not looking at. See [Personal settings](/account/settings#notifications).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the colored banner at the top of my screen?">
    An official Merciv announcement, such as a maintenance notice or a new feature. Its color and icon show whether it's information, a warning, or something urgent. Dismissing it hides it until your next page load; it disappears for good when it expires. Announcements can appear or update while you're working, without a refresh.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
