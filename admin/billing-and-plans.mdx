Your organization's plan is set up with Merciv rather than managed in the app. There's no billing page in Settings — to review or change your plan, request access from inside Merciv or contact your Merciv representative.

## What your plan affects

Plans determine what your organization can access and how much of it:

- **Feature availability.** Some capabilities are included only on select plans — for example, [Knowledge](/knowledge/overview), [Portfolio](/portfolio/overview), image generation in [Galleries](/galleries/overview), and [integrations](/knowledge/integrations). Features your plan doesn't include show a locked state with a **Request Access** option.
- **Credits.** Your organization shares a pool of credits. Chat messages, research, and playbook runs use credits as they run.
- **Seats and members.** Your plan can cap how many people your organization has, and how many [Full Access and Collab seats](/admin/managing-your-organization#seats) you can fill.
- **Trackers.** Each plan allows a set number of [trackers](/trackers/overview).
- **Storage.** How much data your organization can keep in [Knowledge](/knowledge/overview).
- **Integrations.** How many tools each person can connect.

Plan details vary by organization — for specifics on what your plan includes, ask your Merciv representative.

## Trials

If your organization is on a trial, a card in the sidebar counts down the days left, such as "12 days left in trial" or "Trial ends today."

## When you reach a limit

When an action would go past a plan limit, Merciv explains which limit you hit instead of completing it. For example:

- **"You've used all your credits"** — your organization's credits have run out for this cycle, so new messages can't be sent.
- **"Your team has outgrown this plan"** or **"You've filled every seat of this type"** — you can't invite more people, or move someone to that seat type, until a seat frees up.
- **"You're tracking as much as this plan allows"** — you've reached your tracker limit. Archive trackers you no longer need, or ask about a higher limit.
- **"Your knowledge storage is full"** — delete files you no longer need, or ask about more storage.
- **"You've connected as many integrations as this plan allows"** — disconnect a tool you no longer use, or ask about a higher limit.

The dialog may show which plan you're on and how much of the limit you've used. Organization admins and owners see a **Request Access** button that takes you to the Merciv team to discuss more capacity. Other members see a note to contact their organization's administrator.

<Tip>
Removing a pending invite you no longer need frees up its seat — pending invites count toward your seat totals.
</Tip>

## Changing your plan

To upgrade, add seats, or raise a limit, click **Request Access** wherever Merciv offers it, or contact your Merciv representative. The Merciv team works with you to set up the plan that fits your organization.

## If your access lapses

When your organization's plan no longer includes access to Merciv, you'll see **"Request access to continue"** when you open the app. Your work isn't gone — you just can't create new activity until access is restored. If you close the dialog and try to send a message in [Chat](/chat/overview), you'll see **"Upgrade to keep chatting."**

To resolve it:

- Click **Request Access** to reach the Merciv team.
- If you belong to more than one organization, switch to another one from the organization name at the top of the sidebar and keep working there.
- Otherwise, contact your Merciv representative to restore access.
