Artifacts are the saved outputs the assistant produces during a conversation: documents, spreadsheets, presentations, markdown notes, HTML pages, images, videos, PDFs, CSV data files, and interactive Canvas dashboards. Instead of living only inside a chat transcript, each artifact gets its own page where you can view it, download it, edit it, share it with teammates, and browse its version history.

You don't create artifacts manually. When the assistant produces a file or Canvas in a chat, it becomes an artifact automatically. If the chat belongs to a project, the artifact is saved in that project. See [Generated files](/chat/generated-files) for how files come out of chat.

<Note>
Canvas dashboards are available on select plans. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see them.
</Note>

## Open an artifact

- **From chat** — generated files appear as cards on the assistant's message. Click one to open the full artifact page; on desktop, the conversation moves into a side panel so it stays visible. Hover a card to show its **Download** button.
- **From Recents** — the **Artifacts** filter in the sidebar's **Recents** list, or the **Artifacts** tab on the full **Recents** page, shows your artifacts together with your reports.
- **From Home** — on the **Recent** tab, filter by **Artifacts**.
- **From search** — use the **Artifacts** filter in search.
- **From a project** — artifacts saved in a project are listed on the project page.

## View an artifact

The artifact page shows the latest version in a focused viewer, with the file name and actions in the top bar. How it displays depends on the type:

- **Documents, presentations, spreadsheets, and PDFs** open in the built-in document viewer.
- **HTML pages** render as interactive pages.
- **CSV files** open in a data grid.
- **Markdown** renders as a formatted document.
- **Images** display full size, and **videos** play inline.
- **Canvas dashboards** render natively and can be edited in place. See [Edit artifacts](/artifacts/editing).

Click **Fullscreen** in the top bar to give the artifact the whole screen.

For PDFs, Word documents, and presentations, the enhanced document viewer adds:

- **Zoom in**, **Zoom out**, and **Fit page** controls in the bottom-right corner.
- A switch between **Scroll view**, which scrolls continuously, and **Page view**, which shows one page at a time.
- Page thumbnails for jumping to a page in multi-page documents.
- Keyboard paging. Use the Up and Down arrow keys to scroll. In **Page view**, the Left and Right arrow keys and Page Up and Page Down turn pages.
- Cmd/Ctrl+F to find text in the document.

<Note>
The enhanced document viewer is available on select plans. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see these controls.
</Note>

## Artifact actions

The top bar holds the main actions. Which ones you see depends on your access.

- **Version history** (clock icon) — browse and restore earlier versions. See [Version history and restore](/artifacts/editing#version-history-and-restore).
- **Return to chat** — reopens the conversation the artifact came from. It appears only when that conversation is yours, because chats are private.
- **Edit** or **Ask** — opens the chat side panel with the artifact attached. Editors see **Edit** and can ask the assistant for changes. Viewers see **Ask** and can ask questions about it.
- **Share** — opens the share dialog. Editors and the owner only.
- **Download** — downloads the current version. Canvas dashboards have no file to download.

The **More actions** (⋯) menu holds the rest:

- **Rename** — the file extension is kept for you, so you only edit the name.
- **Export as Word doc** — for markdown and plain-text artifacts.
- **Copy content** — copies the text of text-based artifacts.
- **Move to Project** — files the artifact in a project.
- **Delete file** — owner only.

## Share an artifact

The person whose chat produced the artifact is its **Owner**. Editors and the owner can click **Share** to open the share dialog:

- **Invite people** — click **Add emails to invite people**, type a teammate's name or email, and pick them from the suggestions. Choose **Full access** (Editor) or **View only** (Viewer), then click **Invite**. You can only add people in your organization. People you add get a notification.
- **People with access** — lists the owner and everyone you've shared with. Use the dropdown next to a person to switch between **Full access** and **View only**, or choose **Remove access**.
- **Public link** — turn on **Anyone with the link** to let anyone open the artifact without an account. Turning it off shows "The public link no longer grants access," and old links stop working.
- **Copy link** — copies the artifact's link. When the public link is on, it copies the public link.

The public page is read-only. It shows the latest version, with no editing, version history, downloads, or sharing controls.

<Note>
Public artifact links are available on select plans. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see the option.
</Note>

You can't share an artifact with your whole organization at once. Share it with people individually, use the public link, or keep it in a shared project. Artifacts in a project are available to the project's members: project Viewers can view them, and project Editors can edit them. See [Sharing and permissions](/getting-started/sharing-and-permissions).

## Rename, move, and delete

The **...** menu on an artifact in Recents, Home, a project, or search offers **Open** and, depending on your access, **Rename**, **Share**, **Move to Project**, and **Delete**. Editors can rename, share, and move. Only the owner can delete.

Deleting asks you to confirm, then removes the artifact from your lists. If someone opens a deleted artifact from a link or an old chat, they see a banner with the deletion date. The owner sees a **Restore** button on that banner.

## Limits

- Generated files can be up to 100 MB each.
- Canvas dashboards are capped at 2 MB of content; edits beyond that are rejected.
- The version history list shows up to the 50 most recent versions.
