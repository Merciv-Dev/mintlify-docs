"Make sense of it all" is Merciv's core brand idea. The functional value (data chaos → clear intelligence) and the emotional relief (finally seeing the whole picture).

## The device

The **"Make sense of ___"** pattern is a verbal-visual motif used in marketing, social, and brand campaigns. The blank is filled with a specific domain, signal, or challenge:

- Make sense of **consumer behavior as fast as it changes**
- Make sense of **every consumer signal, finally in context**
- Make sense of **the shift before it shows up in your numbers**

## Visual treatment

In the brand toolkit (Figma), this device appears as a typographic composition — display headline in GT Pressura with the variable phrase emphasized. It pairs with the dot-grid background or a clean neutral surface.

## Voice rules

- The blank should be **specific**, not generic — "consumer behavior" not "your data"
- Keep the headline short; the larger the type, the fewer the words
- Never pair this device with both orange and blue accents in the same composition
- No emoji, no hype words — conviction, not salesmanship
