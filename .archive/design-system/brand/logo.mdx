The Merciv wordmark is the primary brand identifier. Use the correct ink variant for the surface background.

## Assets

| File | Ink | Surface |
|---|---|---|
| `merciv-logo-dark-ink.svg` | `#0D0D0C` | Light surfaces (`neutral-98`, `neutral-100`) |
| `merciv-logo-light-ink.svg` | `#FAF9F5` | Dark surfaces (`neutral-10`, `orange-20`, `blue-20`) |

Assets live in `assets/` at the root of the design system folder.

## Usage rules

- Use the **full wordmark** in headers, footers, and closing slides
- On color panels (orange or blue), use the **light-ink** variant with `.on-dark` footer styling
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "M" cap
- Do not recolor, stretch, or add effects to the wordmark
- Do not place the dark-ink wordmark on dark backgrounds or the light-ink wordmark on light backgrounds

## Header placement

On the marketing website, the wordmark sits in the sticky header at left, paired with centered nav and a CTA at right. The header blurs on scroll and hides on scroll-down / reveals on scroll-up.

## Deck usage

On closing slides, the full wordmark appears on a color ground (maroon `orange-20`) with headline, body, and contact block below.
