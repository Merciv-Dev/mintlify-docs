The Merciv symbol is a dot-lattice "M" — dots and rounded squares on a grid. It is the geometric seed of the dot-grid background motif.

## Assets

| File | Ink | Surface |
|---|---|---|
| `merciv-symbol-dark-ink.svg` | `#0D0D0C` | Light surfaces |
| `merciv-symbol-light-ink.svg` | `#FAF9F5` | Dark surfaces |

## Usage

- **App icon / avatar / favicon** — use the symbol mark, not the full wordmark
- **Deck covers** — symbol mark top-right on color ground slides
- **Centered title slides** — symbol top-right alongside mono section number top-left

## Relationship to dot grid

The symbol's dot-lattice structure directly informs the `.bg-dot-grid` background pattern used on heroes and cards. Both use circular dots on a 16px grid — the symbol is the brand distilled to its geometric essence.

## Do not

- Rotate or distort the symbol
- Use the symbol at sizes where individual dots become illegible
- Replace the symbol with emoji or unicode characters
