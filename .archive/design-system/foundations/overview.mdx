## Overall feel

Engineered, calm, structured, modern. Neutrals dominate; accents are rare and deliberate. The aesthetic reads like precision instrumentation, not a hype-y SaaS gradient deck.

## Color philosophy

Grounded in a slightly-warm neutral ramp (`neutral-05` through `neutral-100`). Two brand accents:

- **Orange `#ED4421`** — high-energy
- **Blue/teal `#089DBA`** — calm

<Warning>
  Never mix orange and blue in the same composition. See [Design rules](/rules).
</Warning>

Brand accents are used *sparingly* — heroes, CTAs, one feature family at a time. Semantic colors (success/error/warning/info) are product-UI only, never brand expression.

## Typography

Three families define the hierarchy:

| Family | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| GT Pressura Extended | Display / headlines | Tight negative tracking, tight line-height |
| Die Grotesk A | Body / UI | 0% tracking, 120–150% line-height |
| GT Pressura Mono | Kickers / meta / labels | Uppercase, 0 to +5% tracking |

Hierarchy steps ~1.5–2x; bigger headlines get more whitespace and fewer words.

See [Typography](/foundations/typography) for the full type scale.

## Backgrounds

The signature is a quiet **dot grid** — a 16px radial-dot lattice (`.bg-dot-grid`) that echoes the "M" symbol. Often interactive on heroes, always dissolving at the edges via a linear mask.

Solid brand panels use the darkest shades (`orange-20`, `blue-20`) for full-bleed color sections. No noisy gradients; the *only* gradient is the orange CTA fill.

Real photography sits in rounded **media frames** (`--media-frame-radius: 8px`, clip-only, optional elevated shadow).

## Cards

Subtle by design:

- Fill: `neutral-98` / `neutral-100`
- Radius: 8px
- Border: hairline `rgba(0,0,0,0.06)` via soft two-layer **shadow-as-border** (`--shadow-border`)
- Rest at 90% opacity, lift to 100% on hover
- No colored left-border accents

In dark mode the shadow drops and a translucent white hairline carries the edge.

## Elevation ladder

| Level | Token | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Border | `--shadow-border` | Cards, inputs |
| Floating | `--shadow-floating` | Menus, popovers, tooltips — paired with translucent fill + backdrop-blur |
| Modal | `--shadow-modal` | Dialogs, elevated media |
| CTA | `--shadow-cta-orange` | Branded orange button — vertical gradient + inset highlight + soft drop shadow |

## Borders and dividers

Hairline `neutral-90` dividers (light) / `neutral-30` (dark). Section content separated by thin top-borders rather than heavy boxes.

## Motion

Calm and composite-friendly (opacity + transform only). Canonical easing tokens:

- **snappy** — default entrances/hovers, 200–300ms
- **spring** — subtle overshoot for micro-delight
- **content** — hero/cards
- **exit** — leaving

Signature animations: `fade-in-up` staggered entrances, slow `dot-pulse` on kicker dots, scramble-in reveal on mono labels, edge-faded marquees. Everything honors `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Hover and press states

- **Links:** subtle underline that intensifies, or `text-subtle → text-primary`, 300ms
- **Buttons:** orange CTA lifts (`translateY -1px`) then deepens on hover; press scales to 0.96
- **Tonal/secondary buttons:** shift one fill step
- **Cards:** opacity 90→100 + border darkens slightly
- **Menu rows:** highlight to `neutral-95` (never an offset ring)

## Layout

- Max content container: **1440px**
- Prose column: **672px**
- 12-col grid on marketing sections
- Generous vertical rhythm (`py-12 → py-16 → py-24`)
- Header is fixed/sticky and hides on scroll-down, reveals on scroll-up

## Imagery

Real product UI and warm, true-to-life photography in rounded frames — not illustration, not stock-y gradients, not 3D blobs.
