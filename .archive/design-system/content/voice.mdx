Merciv's voice comes from **conviction, not salesmanship** — a scientist who has spent years studying a problem and finally has something worth sharing. Analytical, institutional but not cold, engineered but accessible, confident without hype, structured and calm.

## Person and casing

- Brand account voice — use "we" sparingly; avoid first-person singular
- **Sentence case** everywhere except mono kicker/meta labels, which are **UPPERCASE** with +0.7px tracking
- Straight quotes, not curly, in product UI
- **No emoji.** Not part of the brand. No hashtag stuffing on social.

## Rhythm

Flowing, layered sentences that build and connect — not staccato fragments. The paragraph completes the arc; a paragraph of buildup earns a two-sentence payoff. The larger the headline, the fewer the words.

## Signature moves

## Specificity over abstraction

Use real numbers and named capabilities. "98% reduction in research cycle time" beats "dramatically faster." Every sentence should be specific enough that it could only be about Merciv.

## Headline voice

> Make sense of consumer behavior as fast as it changes

> Every consumer signal, finally in context

> Spot the shift before it shows up in your numbers

> Built to pass the security review

## Banned words

<Warning>
  Remove these from all Merciv copy.
</Warning>

**Hype:** revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, disruptive, synergy, move the needle, "in today's rapidly evolving landscape," "deep dive" (noun), north star.

**AI tells:** delve, crucial, pivotal, tapestry, testament, underscore, showcase, leverage-as-filler, "not just X but Y" parallelisms, forced rule-of-three, em-dash overuse (max ~2 per piece).
