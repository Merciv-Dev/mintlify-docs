## Website codebase

**[Merciv-Dev/merciv-website](https://github.com/Merciv-Dev/merciv-website)**

Design tokens in `tailwind.config.ts` + `src/app/globals.css`. Marketing components under `src/components/home` and `src/components/ui`. Copy in `src/content/marketing-copy.ts`.

This is the primary source of truth for component implementations.
