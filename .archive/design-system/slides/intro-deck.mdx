The **editorial intro deck** is a minimal, typographic, cream presentation system with no imagery. Open `slides/index.html`.

Source: `Merciv-Decks.fig` → page **EP-Slide-Dump**.

## Canvas

- **Size:** 1920×1080
- **Margins:** 48px on every edge
- **Ground:** cream `neutral-98` (`#FAF9F5`) — not white
- **No dot patterns, no photography** — purely typographic and editorial

## Type roles

| Role | Font | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eyebrow | GT Pressura Mono | 24px | UPPER, +5px tracking, `neutral-40`, top-left @48/48 |
| Headline | GT Pressura | 96px | Line 1.2, −0.03em |
| Cover title | GT Pressura | 120–144px | Line ~0.92, tight |
| Subhead | Die Grotesk | 40px | Card values |
| Body | Die Grotesk | 32px | `neutral-20` |
| Body-sm | Die Grotesk | 24px | Dense rows |
| Index num | GT Pressura Mono | 64px | `neutral-60`, −3px |

GT Pressura is never bold — all headings pinned to weight 400.

## Footer

Every slide has a single-line row of widely-tracked uppercase mono labels pinned to the bottom:

`MERCIV · INTRO DECK · APRIL 2026 · CONFIDENTIAL`

Cells never wrap. Dark on cream, cream (`.on-dark`) on color slides.

## Templates (in order)

1. **Cover** — color ground (orange), 144px title, symbol mark top-right, partner/date
2. **Section divider** — color ground (teal-dark), numbered eyebrow + big statement
3. **Content** — eyebrow + 96px headline + lead paragraph
4. **Big statement** — oversized headline, the "reframe" voice
5. **Overview** — three stat cards (label + 96px value + note) over a full-width callout panel
6. **Guiding principles** — title left, numbered hairline rows right
7. **Use cases** — three tall callout cards (label + 40px value + body)
8. **Timeline** — horizontal stops (eyebrow + 48px title + body)
9. **Closing** — color ground (maroon), full wordmark, headline + body + contact block

## Cover color variants

Three grounds ship in Figma:

| Ground | Token | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Orange | `orange-50` | Primary cover |
| Teal-dark | `blue-20` | Section dividers |
| Maroon | `orange-20` | Closing slide |

Swap the slide `background` and use `.on-dark` footer + the light-ink logo. **Never put a brand accent on a content (cream) slide.**

## Cards and panels

8px radius, `neutral-95` fill, `neutral-90` hairline border, 48px padding. Numbered lists use hairline top-borders (no fill).

## Navigation

Both decks share `deck-stage.js` — arrow keys, thumbnails, and print-to-PDF.
