These five rules govern every Merciv interface, deck, and marketing asset. Breaking any of them produces off-brand work.

## 1. Never mix orange and blue

Orange (`#ED4421`) and blue/teal (`#089DBA`) are both brand accents, but they **never appear in the same composition**.

Pair a hue within its own family or with neutrals. On a blue panel, use blue tints and neutrals. On an orange panel, use orange tints and neutrals.

<Warning>
  This is the single most important color rule in the system.
</Warning>

## 2. Neutrals dominate

The warm neutral ramp (`neutral-05` through `neutral-100`) is the foundation of every layout. Brand accents are **sparse and deliberate** — heroes, CTAs, one feature family at a time.

Avoid using opacity in place of a real neutral step. The only gradient in the system is the orange CTA fill.

## 3. Never bold GT Pressura

GT Pressura ships as **Regular only**. Bolding synthesizes a fake weight that looks wrong.

Pin all display headings to `font-weight: 400`. Use size, tracking, and whitespace for hierarchy instead.

```css
.type-display {
  font-family: var(--font-display);
  font-weight: 400; /* never 700 */
}
```

## 4. Dot grid = circles, one step off the surface

The signature dot-grid background uses a **16px radial-dot lattice** (`.bg-dot-grid`). Dots are always circular and sit one neutral step off their surface:

- `95` on `98`
- `90` on `95`
- `20` on `10`
- `30` on its matching `20` panel

Always edge-fade the grid with a linear mask so it never competes with content.

## 5. No hype, no emoji, no AI-tells

Merciv's voice comes from **conviction, not salesmanship**. Speed is proof of depth — never the headline.

**Banned words:** revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, disruptive, synergy, move the needle, "in today's rapidly evolving landscape," "deep dive" (noun), north star.

**AI tells to remove:** delve, crucial, pivotal, tapestry, testament, underscore, showcase, leverage-as-filler, "not just X but Y" parallelisms, forced rule-of-three, em-dash overuse (max ~2 per piece).

**Emoji:** none. Not part of the brand.
