The Consumer Signal Poster turns one consumer-intelligence insight into a shareable, gallery-grade square object.

Entry point: `templates/consumer-signal-poster/ConsumerSignalPoster.dc.html`

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Focus |
|---|---|
| **A — Brand** | Single-brand insight with hero number |
| **C — Head-to-head** | Competitive comparison |
| **D — Cultural** | Cultural trend or shift |

## Customization

Drop in:

- Brand name and logo
- Hero number or stat
- Source citation
- Supporting context copy

## Format

- Square (1:1) poster layout
- Brand tokens from `colors_and_type.css` + `fonts.css`
- Merciv wordmark in footer
- Full source attribution

## Voice

Posters follow Merciv content rules — specificity over abstraction, real numbers, no banned words. The insight should be specific enough that it could only be about one brand or category.

See [Content voice](/content/voice) and [Design rules](/rules).
