The Consumer Signal Carousel is a LinkedIn depth format (archetype E): 6 square slides in one visual system.

Entry point: `templates/signal-carousel/SignalCarousel.dc.html`

## Slide sequence

1. **Cover hook** — attention-grabbing headline on brand ground
2. **The shift** — what changed in the market or consumer behavior
3. **The driver** — what's causing the shift
4. **The comparison** — before/after or competitive context
5. **The so-what** — implications for the brand
6. **Sources + CTA** — cited sources and call to action

## Format

- Square slides (1:1 aspect ratio)
- Single visual system across all 6 slides
- Full source attribution on the final slide
- Brand tokens from `colors_and_type.css` + `fonts.css`

## Usage

Drop in your insight, supporting data, and source citations. The template handles layout, type hierarchy, and brand expression.

Each slide follows Merciv voice rules — specific, calm, confident. No hype, no emoji.

See [Content voice](/content/voice) for copy guidelines.
