Merciv is a **consumer intelligence platform** for enterprise consumer brands. This design system captures the brand's visual language, voice, and UI patterns — reconstructed from the production website, brand toolkit, and Figma files.

**Core brand idea:** Make sense of it all.

## Platform at a glance

Merciv unifies three data layers into one intelligence layer:

- **Internal** data (Looker, Snowflake, Databricks, SAP)
- **Syndicated** data (NielsenIQ, Circana, Euromonitor, Kantar, SPINS, Mintel)
- **External** data (TikTok, Reddit, Instagram, X, Amazon reviews, Google Trends)

## The five rules

<Warning>
  These rules are non-negotiable. See [Design rules](/rules) for full detail.
</Warning>

1. **Never mix orange and blue** in one composition.
2. **Neutrals dominate** — accents are sparse and deliberate.
3. **Never bold GT Pressura** — display headings stay at `font-weight: 400`.
4. **Dot grid = circles**, one step off the surface, always edge-faded.
5. **No hype, no emoji, no AI-tells** — speed is proof of depth, never the headline.
