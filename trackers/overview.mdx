A tracker is an always-on research assistant. You describe what to watch — a topic, a set of competitors, a market, a regulation — and the tracker researches it on a recurring schedule (or on demand) and publishes its findings as **stories**: readable research articles that appear on the tracker's page and in [Discover](/discover/overview).

Instead of re-asking the same research question every week, you configure it once and Merciv delivers fresh, non-duplicative updates automatically.

Open **Trackers** from the main navigation sidebar. Your trackers appear as cards, organized into tabs:

- **Private** — your personal trackers (the default view).
- **Organization** — trackers shared with your whole organization. Visible to organization admins only.
- **Archived** — trackers you've archived.

Use the sort menu to switch between **Most Recent** and **Oldest**. Click a card to open the tracker's page, which shows its topic, status, next run time, sources, and a timeline of runs and the stories each run produced.

## Create a tracker

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new tracker">
    On the Trackers page, click **New**. If the tab you're on has no trackers yet, you can also pick one of three starter templates — **Competitive Brand Monitoring** (weekly), **Industry News & Trends** (weekly), or **Market & Financial Signals** (monthly) — which pre-fill the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and write a monitoring brief">
    Give the tracker a name, then describe what it should watch in the topic field. Be specific — for example: "Monitor quarterly earnings and guidance from major sneaker brands including Nike, Adidas, and New Balance." Both fields are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a schedule">
    Pick a **Frequency**: **Manual**, **Daily**, **Weekly** (the default), or **Monthly**.

    - **Daily** — choose a time of day.
    - **Weekly** — choose one or more days of the week and a time.
    - **Monthly** — choose a day of the month (1–31) and a time.

    Times are offered in 30-minute increments and run in your browser's timezone, which is shown next to the time picker. A new tracker defaults to Weekly, Monday at 9:00 AM.

    Manual trackers never run on their own — they only run when you click **Run Now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add sources (optional)">
    Enter a website URL and click **Add** to focus the research on specific sites. You can add up to 10. Leave the list empty to search broadly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. The tracker appears in your list and runs on its schedule from then on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Write a concrete monitoring brief. A run with an empty or placeholder topic (like "test") is rejected and recorded as a failed run.
</Tip>

## Personal and organization trackers

- **Personal trackers** (the **Private** tab) are visible and manageable only by you. Any organization member can create one, and its stories are private to you unless you share them.
- **Organization trackers** are visible and manageable only by organization admins. Their stories are visible to everyone in the organization.

If you're an organization admin, the create form includes **My Tracker** and **Organization Tracker** scope tabs. Admins can also convert a tracker's scope later — see [Managing trackers](/trackers/managing-trackers).

<Note>
  Organization trackers are available on select plans with more than one user.
</Note>

## Create a tracker from chat

You can also set up trackers without leaving a conversation. When you ask Merciv to "track", "monitor", or "keep me updated on" something in [Chat](/chat/overview), it can propose one to three trackers. The proposals appear one at a time in a card showing the name, monitoring brief, schedule, and sources.

- Click **Create** to set up the tracker, or **Skip** to pass on it.
- Use **Next** to move to the next proposal, and **Done** when you've decided on all of them.

Trackers created from chat are personal trackers scheduled at 9:00 AM in your timezone (Mondays for weekly trackers, the 1st for monthly ones). To change the brief, schedule, or sources, edit the tracker on the Trackers page afterward — or ask Merciv for a revised proposal. Once you're done, the conversation shows which trackers were created, each linking to the Trackers page.

## What a run produces

Each run is an AI research job. Merciv researches your topic (or generates fresh sub-topics for the period), checks the candidates against stories the tracker has already published so you only get genuinely new material, and writes a story for each new topic — a research article with citations, a title, a summary, and a cover illustration. Processing happens in the background and can take a few minutes.

Finished stories appear on the tracker's page and in [Discover](/discover/overview).
