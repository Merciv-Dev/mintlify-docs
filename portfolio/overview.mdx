Portfolio is a dashboard for organizations that track a product portfolio. It organizes everything into a brand → product → variant hierarchy and pairs each level with dashboards of metric widgets, so you can see how your portfolio is performing in one place.

<Note>
Portfolio is available on select plans. If you don't see **Portfolio** in the sidebar, or it shows a lock icon, click **Request Access** on the Portfolio page or contact your Merciv representative.
</Note>

## Browse your portfolio

The left sidebar lists your **Brands**, each with its logo and a count badge. Expand a brand to see its products, and a product to see its variants. Click the search icon to search across brands, products, and variants by name — results show where each item sits in the hierarchy.

Selecting a brand, product, or variant shows an overview card with its image, name, and key details:

- Click a **brand's** card to open **Manage brand**, with a **Catalog** tab for browsing the brand's products and listings and a **Sources** tab listing the retailers and sites its data comes from.
- Click a **product's** or **variant's** card to open a detail panel listing its variants or listings.

The top bar includes a button to hide the sidebar, breadcrumbs, and back/forward arrows that walk your navigation history within the page.

## Dashboards and time filters

Each level of the hierarchy has its own dashboards, organized as tabs. A dashboard is a grid of widgets — bar, line, pie, radar, heatmap, table, single-metric, gauge, and more.

Use the time-range filter to scope the data: **Past 30 days**, **Past 60 days**, **Past 90 days**, **Past 6 months**, or **Past Year**.

Users with portfolio management access can create dashboards with **+**, delete them from a tab's **...** menu, and change a dashboard's widgets with the edit button.

## Add a brand

Adding and managing brands requires portfolio management access, which is limited to organization admins. Users without it can browse the portfolio but won't see the create and edit controls.

Click **+** in the Brands sidebar and choose **Create brand**. A short guided setup walks you through the rest:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a brand">
    Enter the **Brand name** (required) and the brand's **Website**, and choose your **Relationship** to the brand: **My brand**, **Client**, **Prospect**, **Competitor**, or **Reference**. Click **Create brand**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Merciv enrich the profile">
    If you added a website, Merciv builds the brand's profile from it. This takes a moment; click **Review** when it's ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and validate">
    Check the enriched profile — description, categories, suggested competitors, social profiles, and brand narrative such as tagline, value proposition, tone and voice, audiences, and key messages. Adjust anything, then validate the brand to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm sources">
    Merciv suggests the places the brand sells. Confirm the ones you want — nothing is collected until you do. You can also add a source yourself: choose **Amazon**, **Walmart**, **Target**, **Ulta Beauty**, **Sephora**, or **Brand website**, paste the storefront URL, and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Each source gathers the brand's catalog and reviews once. While that runs, the portfolio shows "Processing brand — We are processing the brand information. This may take a few minutes." You can turn on ongoing collection later from the brand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your plan may limit how many brands your organization can add.

## Manage a brand

Portfolio managers can come back to a brand at any time:

- **Review brand** — brands that are ready for review show a review button in the sidebar. Open it to edit the profile and validate the brand.
- **Manage brand** — click the brand's overview card. On **Catalog**, browse, rename, or delete products and open their listings. On **Sources**, add or remove retailer storefronts and the brand's site. **Delete brand** removes the brand and everything attached to it, and can't be undone.

## Add listings

To add specific product pages, select a variant and open its detail panel, then click **New Listing**. In **Add New Listings**, choose the store, paste one or more product-page URLs, and click **Add New Listings**. New listings are processed in the background and added shortly.

<Tip>
If adding listings fails with "Please check the URLs and try again," confirm each URL is a complete product-page link for the store you selected.
</Tip>

## Managing access

Portfolio access follows your organization's plan and roles — see [Managing your organization](/admin/managing-your-organization) for how admin roles work. If your team needs portfolio management access for additional people, contact your Merciv representative.
