Two controls in the composer shape how the assistant handles your question. The **mode** picker, next to the send button, sets how deeply the assistant works. The **Create** button, at the left, sets what it produces.

## Modes

Click the mode picker to switch. Hover over a mode to see what it does and roughly how long it takes. Your choice applies to every message in that chat until you change it.

| Mode | What it does | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Ask** | Gives a cited answer in seconds, in a single pass over the top-ranked sources. | Lookups and follow-ups where speed matters more than depth. |
| **Work** | The default. Searches your connected sources and checks its findings before answering. Usually takes about 30 seconds. | Most questions, especially ones that need evidence from several sources. |
| **Deep research** | Breaks your question into parts, researches them in parallel, and returns a sourced report you can trace end to end. Usually takes 5 to 15 minutes. | Broad questions that deserve a thorough, citable document, such as competitive landscapes, category reviews, or trend analyses. |
| **Personas** | Puts your question to one or more of your organization's personas, like a research panel. | Testing how a message, concept, or product lands with a specific audience. |

<Note>
**Personas** mode is available on select plans. When it's available, choose it and then pick personas from the list. Select **All personas** to ask the whole panel.
</Note>

Deep research keeps going in the background, so you can leave the page while it runs. Turn on notifications to hear when it's done (see [Chat overview](/chat/overview#notifications-and-status)). The finished report appears as a report card in the message, sometimes a few seconds after the answer text.

## Output formats

Click **Create** and choose what the assistant should produce:

- **Report**: a structured written document.
- **Dashboard**: metrics at a glance.
- **Slides**: a presentation deck.
- **Spreadsheet**: tables and formulas you can download and work with.
- **Visualization**: charts and diagrams.
- **Canvas**: an interactive dashboard shown right in the answer, with charts, tables, metric tiles, and drilldowns you can explore in place.

The format you pick shows as a pill in the composer. Click the **X** on the pill to go back to letting the assistant decide. The format resets after each message, so pick it again if you want the next answer in the same format.

<Note>
The **Create** button and **Canvas** are available on select plans. Without them, the assistant picks the best format for each question. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see an option described here.
</Note>

Generated documents, spreadsheets, and slides appear as file cards in the answer. See [Generated files](/chat/generated-files) for how to open, copy, and download them.

## Voice dictation

In browsers that support speech recognition, a microphone button next to the mode picker turns your speech into text in the composer. If the browser blocks the microphone or doesn't hear anything, a "Voice input" message explains what happened.

<Note>
Dictation is available on select plans. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see the microphone button.
</Note>
