title: "Sharing and exporting reports"
description: "Share reports with teammates, publish a public link, and export to PDF or Word."
icon: "share-2"
---

Open the share dialog from the **Share** button in the report's top bar, or from **Share** in the top bar's **...** menu. The button is visible to users with **Full access** (Editor) or Owner permission on the report.

## Share with teammates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start an invite">
    In the share dialog, click **Add emails to invite people**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people">
    Type a teammate's name or email and pick them from the suggestions. You can also paste a list of emails. Only members of your organization can be invited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose access and invite">
    Choose **Full access** (Editor) or **View only** (Viewer), then click **Invite**. Each person gets a notification linking to the report.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What each level allows:

- **View only** (Viewer) — read and export the report, but not edit or share it
- **Full access** (Editor) — edit the report, rename it, move it to a project, and manage sharing
- **Owner** — the report's creator; full control, including delete

The **People with access** list shows everyone who can open the report. Use the dropdown next to a person to switch between **Full access** and **View only**, or choose **Remove access**. If someone's access is removed while they have the report open, they see a notice and are returned home.

<Note>
Reports are shared per person or via the public link. There is no option to share a report with your entire organization at once.
</Note>

## Publish a public link

The **Public link** section of the share dialog has an **Anyone with the link** switch.

- Turn it on to publish the report. You'll see "Report is now public." Click **Copy link** at the bottom of the dialog to copy the public link. Anyone who opens it can read the report without a Merciv account. Public visitors get read-only access to the report and the sources it cites; they can never edit.
- Turn it off to make the report private again. You'll see "Report is now private — The public link no longer grants access." The link stops working immediately.

<Warning>
Turning off the public link revokes it for everyone, instantly. If you turn it back on later, Merciv issues a new link — the old one never works again. Re-share the new link with anyone who needs it.
</Warning>

## Export to PDF or Word

Click the download icon in the report's top bar and choose:

- **PDF** — you'll see "PDF Exported!" when it completes
- **Word Doc** — you'll see "Report Exported!" when it completes

Anyone who can open the report can export it. Exports include the current document content.

<Tip>
If an export fails, try the other format. Very large or unusually formatted documents occasionally fail in one format but succeed in the other.
</Tip>
