Merciv is organized around organizations. Everything you create lives inside your organization, and you join one through an invitation, an approved email domain, or an invite code.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the signup page">
    Open Merciv and click **Sign Up**. If a teammate sent you an invite link with a code, use their link — the code is applied automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use your work email">
    Merciv is available for work email addresses only. If you try a personal address (such as a Gmail account), you'll see **Use your work email**. Click **Try another account** and sign up with your company email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Merciv sends a verification link to your address and holds you at a **Check Your Email** screen. Click the link in the email, then click **I've Verified My Email** to continue. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder or try signing up again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    After your first login, Merciv walks you through a short questionnaire before you can use the app. See [Onboarding](#onboarding) below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you open a Merciv link from inside another app (such as LinkedIn, Instagram, or Facebook), login opens as a full-page redirect instead of a popup. This is expected.
</Note>

## How you join an organization

After you authenticate, Merciv checks how your account should join:

- **Email invite.** If an organization admin invited your email address, the invite is accepted automatically and you land in that organization. This also works if you already have an account — pending invites are accepted at your next login.
- **Approved email domain.** Organizations can approve their company email domains. If your work email matches an approved domain, you join that organization automatically as a member.
- **Invite code.** A personal invite code or link from another Merciv user lets you through signup and attributes your signup to them. If you also have a pending email invite, the email invite takes precedence — you join the inviting organization.
- **Self-service signup.** Some customers can sign up without an invitation, which creates a new organization with them as its owner and admin.

<Note>
Self-service signup is available for select customers. If you don't have an invitation and can't sign up on your own, contact your Merciv representative.
</Note>

## Onboarding

New users complete a short questionnaire before entering the app. Every page redirects to onboarding until it's finished.

- **Organization owners** answer four questions: your name, company name, industry, and role.
- **Members** joining an existing organization answer two: your name and role.

Industry and role are pickers with an "Other" option. Some steps can be skipped; name and company cannot. After the questions, a short personalized chat starts to tailor your experience — if it fails to load, click **Try Again** or skip it to finish.

Everything you enter here can be changed later in [Settings](/account/settings), and organization details can be updated by an admin in [organization settings](/admin/managing-your-organization).

## If you land on the waitlist

If you sign up without an invite, a valid invite code, or a matching approved domain, you'll see **"You're on the waitlist!"** — access to Merciv is by invitation. To get in:

- **Check the email address.** Invites are tied to the exact address they were sent to. Make sure you authenticated with the same email your admin invited (watch for aliases and typos).
- **Ask an organization admin to invite you.** Admins invite members from **Settings → Members**.
- **Use an invite link.** If a teammate shares their personal invite link, sign up through it so the code is applied.
- Still stuck? Contact your Merciv representative.

## Trouble logging in

- **Stuck on "Check Your Email":** you need to click the verification link before you can enter the app. Check spam, click the link, then **I've Verified My Email**.
- **Bounced back to the login page:** the app clears the partial session automatically so your next attempt starts clean. Try logging in again.
- **Logged in but no organization:** if you were removed from your only organization, you can still log in but can't work in a workspace. Ask an admin for a new invite.

For more, see [Common issues](/troubleshooting/common-issues).
