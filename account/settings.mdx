Your account menu is the button with your name and avatar at the bottom of the left sidebar. Click it and choose **Settings** to open your settings. The **Personal** group covers everything about your own account. If you're an organization admin or owner, you'll also see an **Organization** group — see [Managing your organization](/admin/managing-your-organization).

<Note>
Some sections below appear only on select plans or for select accounts. If you don't see one, contact your Merciv representative.
</Note>

## The account menu

Besides **Settings**, the account menu gives you quick access to:

- **Invite** (or **Invite teammates**) — bring colleagues into Merciv. See [Invites](#invites).
- **Members** — for organization admins and owners, a shortcut to the member list.
- **Integrations** — connect the tools your team already uses. See [Integrations](/knowledge/integrations).
- **Help** — the changelog, a way to send feedback to the Merciv team, and, where available, documentation.
- A **Light** / **Dark** / **System** theme switch.
- **Sign out**.

Your name in the menu shows your role in the current organization, such as **MEMBER**, **ADMIN**, or **OWNER**.

## Profile

Your profile is what Merciv knows about you, and much of it was captured during onboarding:

- **Account Email** — shown read-only; this is the address you log in with.
- **Name** and **Photo**.
- **Role**, **Department**, and **Responsibilities** (select at least one).
- **Preferred Data Types** — the kinds of data you care about most, such as competitor data, pricing data, product data, market trends, consumer insights, sales data, or campaign data.

Click **Save Changes** and Merciv confirms with "Profile updated." Click **Cancel** to discard your edits.

## Appearance

Choose your **App Theme**: **System Default**, **Light**, or **Dark**. System Default follows your computer's theme. Your choice is saved in the browser you're using, so set it again if you switch devices.

<Tip>
The account menu has the same theme switch, so you don't need to open Settings to change themes.
</Tip>

## Notifications

### Chat reply notifications

Turn on **Chat reply notifications** to get a heads-up when Merciv replies or asks you a question in a chat you're not looking at. A notice appears in the app with an **Open** button that takes you to the chat. If Merciv is in a background tab, you also get a desktop notification.

This setting is off by default and applies to the browser you're using. When you turn it on, your browser asks for permission to show notifications. If notifications are blocked for Merciv in your browser, you'll see "Can't enable notifications" — allow them in your browser's site settings, then try again.

<Tip>
You can also turn this on while a long answer is running: click **Notify me** on the hint above the chat box, then keep working in other chats.
</Tip>

### Email notifications

Control which email notifications you receive with per-category toggles. Changes save immediately — you'll see "Preference updated" when a toggle takes effect. If no categories are listed, you'll see "No email notification preferences available at this time."

## Invites

Your personal invite link lets you bring colleagues into Merciv. Anyone who signs up with your code or link is attributed to you. Copy the **Share link** and send it directly — the recipient signs up through it and the code is applied automatically. See [Getting access](/account/getting-access) for what happens on their end.

Organization admins can also send email invitations — see [Managing your organization](/admin/managing-your-organization#inviting-people).

## Integrations

Integrations has its own page, opened from the account menu → **Integrations**. There you can connect the tools your team already uses so Merciv can work with them in chat, and organization admins can sync files from cloud storage into Knowledge. See [Integrations](/knowledge/integrations) for the full guide.

<Note>
Integrations are available on select plans, and each plan sets how many connections each person can add. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see Integrations in your account menu.
</Note>
