Discover is your reading hub for **stories** — research articles that Merciv generates automatically from your [tracker](/trackers/overview) runs. You set up a tracker once, and finished stories land in Discover on its schedule.

Open **Discover** from the main navigation sidebar, directly below Home. You need to be signed in to use Discover.

## Stories

A story is an AI-generated research article produced by a tracker run. Each story includes a title, a short summary, a cover illustration, the full article body with inline citations, an activity panel showing the research behind it, and topic tags. Stories are read-only — you read them, share them, and spin follow-up work off them in chat.

## Browse your stories

Discover shows the stories you have access to in your active organization, newest first. The most recent story appears as a large banner at the top; the rest render as a grid of cards. More stories load automatically as you scroll.

Tabs at the top narrow the list:

- **All** (default) — every story you can see: stories from your own trackers, stories shared with your whole organization, and stories shared with you directly.
- **Private** — only stories generated by your own personal trackers.
- **Organization** — stories your whole organization can read, such as those from organization trackers.

Switching organizations refreshes Discover for the new organization.

An empty Discover isn't an error — stories from your trackers appear here as they're generated. If it's empty, check that you have active trackers and that their runs are completing on the [Trackers page](/trackers/managing-trackers).

<Note>
  Stories with a public link turned on don't appear under **All** or **Private**. They stay reachable through their link, and under **Organization** if they're also shared with your organization.
</Note>

## Read a story

Click any story to open the full article. The bar under the summary shows the tracker that produced the story (if you can open that tracker), who can see the story (**Public**, **Organization**, **Shared**, or **Private**), and when it was published. Click the cover image to view it full size. Topic tags appear at the bottom of the article.

From the story page you can:

- **Summarize** — opens the chat panel with a new chat scoped to the story, pre-loaded with "Summarize this story".
- **Track** — starts a chat that helps you build a new tracker based on the story, so one interesting article becomes an ongoing monitor.
- **Share** — choose **Manage access** to open the share dialog, or **Copy Link** to copy the story's URL.
- **More Stories** — a grid at the bottom of the page with up to four other recent stories.

<Note>
  The Track action is available on select plans and on desktop only. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see it.
</Note>

## Share a story

Open the share dialog from **Share** → **Manage access**, or by clicking the visibility label on the story.

- **Invite people** — click **Add emails to invite people**, enter teammates from your organization, choose **View only** (Viewer) or **Full access** (Editor), and click **Invite**. They get a notification linking to the story.
- **General access** — organization admins can switch a story between **Only people invited** and **Anyone in the organization**.
- **Public link** — organization admins can turn on **Anyone with the link** to make the story viewable without a Merciv account. Turning it off revokes access for everyone with the link immediately.

Only people with edit access can share a story; others see "You do not have permission to share this item." See [Sharing and permissions](/getting-started/sharing-and-permissions).

## Delete a story

The owner of a story from a personal tracker can delete it from the **...** menu on the story page. Organization admins can delete stories shared with the whole organization. Deleting a story can't be undone.
