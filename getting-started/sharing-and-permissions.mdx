Everything you create in Merciv lives inside your organization and is **private to you by default**. Teammates never see your items until you share them. From there, sharing works along three axes: per-user sharing, organization-wide sharing, and public links.

## Per-user sharing

Projects, reports, stories, and artifacts share through the same dialog. Galleries share the same way once they're inside a project. To share an item:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share dialog">
    Choose **Share** from the item's **...** menu, or click **Share** on a project page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people">
    Click **Add emails to invite people**, then type or paste teammates' email addresses, or pick from the suggestions as you type. Only members of your organization can be added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their access and invite">
    Pick **Full access** or **View only** from the access menu, then click **Invite**. New invites default to **Full access**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dialog shows two access levels, plus the owner:

- **Viewer** (shown as **View only**) — can open and read the item.
- **Editor** (shown as **Full access**) — can also rename and modify it, move it to a project, and share it with others.
- **Owner** — the creator; has full control, including delete. The owner's access can't be changed or removed.

Only editors and owners can share an item — viewers see "You do not have permission to share this item." Under **People with access**, editors and owners can switch others between **Full access** and **View only**, or choose **Remove access**.

**Copy link** at the bottom of the dialog copies the item's address. The link only works for people who already have access, unless you also turn on a [public link](#public-links).

When you share something, the recipient gets a notification in their **Inbox** linking to the item.

### Project access levels

Projects use the same model with project-specific effects:

- **Owner** — everything, including deleting the project.
- **Editor** — rename the project, edit its description and cover, create items inside it, and share it.
- **Viewer** — read-only; no Share button, no editing.

Sharing a project also shares the reports, galleries, and artifacts inside it. Project viewers can view them, and project editors can edit them. Only each item's own owner can delete it.

**Chats are the exception.** A chat stays private to the person who created it, even inside a shared project. See [Projects](/projects/overview).

## Organization-wide sharing

Two places support making something visible to your whole organization:

- **Stories** — organization admins see a **General access** setting in a story's share dialog. Switch it from **Only people invited** to **Anyone in the organization** to give everyone in the organization view access.
- **Knowledge** — files, folders, and knowledge bases have their own organization-level sharing controls. See [Organizing and sharing](/knowledge/organizing-and-sharing).

Projects, chats, reports, galleries, and artifacts do not have an organization-wide option — they are shared per-user only.

## Public links

Some items can be shared outside your organization with a view-only link that works without a Merciv account. In the share dialog, turn on **Anyone with the link** under **Public link**:

- **Reports** — any editor or owner can make a report public. See [Sharing and exporting reports](/reports/sharing-and-exporting).
- **Stories** — only organization admins can make a story public.
- **Artifacts** — any editor or owner can make an artifact public.

Turning the switch off makes the item private again immediately: the public link no longer grants access. Public-link visitors can only view — they can never edit.

<Note>
  Public artifact links are available on select plans. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see the option.
</Note>

## What can't be shared

**Chats are always private to their creator.** There is no share option on a chat, and no one else — including organization admins or members of a shared project — can open your conversations. To share the outcome of a chat, share the artifact it produced or copy its content into a [report](/reports/overview).

## Roles in your organization

- **Member** — the default role. Full product use, personal settings, and per-user sharing of items they own.
- **Organization admin** — everything a member can do, plus organization settings: inviting and removing members, toggling other members' admin status, managing approved email domains, branding, billing, organization-wide story sharing, and public story links.
- **Primary admin (owner)** — the organization's designated owner, with guaranteed billing access.

Admins manage members and roles from **Members** in the account menu, or under **Settings → Members**. See [Managing your organization](/admin/managing-your-organization). Note that admin status doesn't bypass item privacy — a member's private items stay private.

## Good to know

- **Access changes apply in near-real time.** Someone who loses access to an open item may be redirected the next time it loads.
- **Opening a link without access** shows "No access — You don't have access to this project" (or the equivalent for the item type) and returns you to Home. Ask the owner to share it with you.
- **Sharing is organization-scoped.** Per-user sharing only reaches members of your organization; use public links to reach anyone else.
- **Deleting can't be undone in the app.** Deleted projects, chats, reports, galleries, and artifacts disappear from all lists. If you delete something by mistake, email [hello@merciv.com](mailto:hello@merciv.com) as soon as possible.
