## Home

Home is your landing page after login. Type a question into the prompt box and press Enter to start a chat — it opens full screen. Click a prompt suggestion below the box to pre-fill it, or use **Work in a project** under the box to start the chat inside one of your projects.

Below the prompt box, tabs organize your work:

- **Recent** — a carousel of your projects, then your recent work (reports, galleries, chats, and artifacts) grouped by day, with filter and sort controls.
- **Playbooks** — your organization's playbook catalog.
- **Projects** — every project you can access, most recent first.

See [Home and projects](/projects/overview) for details.

<Note>
  Playbooks are available on select plans. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see the tab.
</Note>

## Left sidebar

The sidebar is always visible on desktop. Use the button next to your organization's name to collapse it to icons, and again to expand it. On mobile, open it with the navigation button at the left of the top bar. From top to bottom:

- **Organization** — your active organization. If you belong to more than one, click its name to switch.
- **Search** — opens global search (see below).
- **Home** — the landing page.
- **Discover** — the reading feed for published stories.
- **Trackers** — create and manage your scheduled research agents.
- **Playbooks** — pre-built guided workflows.
- **Personas** — research-backed people you can chat with and study.
- **Portfolio** — the product-intelligence dashboard.
- **Skills** — reusable instructions Merciv uses when relevant, or that you trigger with **/** in chat.
- **Knowledge** — your document library.
- **Projects** — your five most recent projects. Click **+** to create a project, or click the **Projects** heading to see all of them.
- **Recents** — your ten most recent items, grouped by day. Use the filter button to show **All**, **Chats**, or **Artifacts**, or click the **Recents** heading to open the full [Recents page](#recents-page).

At the bottom of the sidebar are your account button (see [Account menu](#account-menu)) and the **Inbox** button.

A colored dot next to a chat means it's still working, is waiting for your input, or has a reply you haven't read yet. Hover the dot to see which.

<Note>
  Playbooks, Personas, Portfolio, Skills, and Knowledge are available on select plans. Items your plan doesn't include may be hidden or show a lock icon. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see one you expect.
</Note>

## Account menu

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu:

- **Settings** — your profile, appearance, and notification preferences. Organization admins also see organization settings here. See [Account settings](/account/settings).
- **Integrations** — connect the other tools your team uses.
- **Invite** — invite teammates to your organization.
- **Members** — manage your organization's members (organization admins only).
- **Help** — **Changelog**, **Give Feedback**, and **Documentation** where available.
- A **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** theme switch.
- **Sign out**.

<Note>
  Integrations are available on select plans. Contact your Merciv representative if you don't see the option.
</Note>

## Top bar

Every page has a top bar with breadcrumbs on the left. On the right:

- A **...** actions menu for the current item. Depending on your access, it offers **Rename**, **Share**, **Move to Project**, and **Delete**.
- The **New** button — a dropdown to create a **New Project**, **New Chat**, **New Report**, or **New Gallery**. (New Project is hidden while you're already inside a project; New Gallery appears on select plans.)
- A **chat** button that opens a new chat in a side panel (see below).

The same **...** menu appears on items in lists, cards, and the sidebar.

## Global search

Press **Cmd/Ctrl+K** anywhere — or click **Search** at the top of the sidebar — to open the command palette.

- Search matches item **names** across your active organization: your own items plus anything shared with you. It covers reports, galleries, chats, artifacts, projects, stories, and Knowledge files and folders.
- Use the filter dropdown to narrow results to **Reports**, **Galleries**, **Chats**, **Artifacts**, or **Projects**.
- Before you type, the palette shows your 15 most recent items.
- Each result shows the item's name, type, its project (if any), and when it was last updated. Click to open it.
- Can't remember the name? Click **Find with Agent** under the search box. Merciv starts a chat that searches through your recent work for what you described.

## Recents page

The Recents page collects everything you've worked on, newest first and grouped by day. Open it by clicking the **Recents** heading in the sidebar.

- Tabs switch between **All**, **Chats**, **Artifacts** (artifacts and reports), and **Projects**.
- Sort by **Most Recent**, **Oldest**, or **Alphabetical**.
- The search button opens global search, filtered to the tab you're on.

## Chat: side panel or full screen

Chat runs in two layouts:

- **Side panel** — the chat button in the top bar opens a resizable panel next to whatever page you're on, so you can chat while looking at a report or dashboard.
- **Full screen** — chats you start from Home, or open from Home or the Recents page, open as full pages.

Use **Move to side panel** and **Expand to full screen** on a chat to switch between the two. If you open a report, artifact, or project while a full-screen chat is running, the chat moves to the side panel so you don't lose it.

On mobile, the side panel is unavailable — chat always opens full screen.

## Where your work lives

- **Recents** — everything you've touched lately, in the sidebar and on the Recents page.
- **Projects** — containers that group related chats, reports, galleries, and artifacts under one name, with per-user sharing. Use **Move to Project** from any item's **...** menu to file it. See [Home and projects](/projects/overview).
- **Knowledge** — your uploaded documents. See [Organizing and sharing](/knowledge/organizing-and-sharing).
- **Discover** — stories published by your trackers and your organization. See [Discover](/discover/overview).

## Inbox and notifications

Click the **Inbox** button at the bottom of the sidebar — or press **Cmd/Ctrl+I** — to see your notifications. A badge shows your unread count. You're notified when:

- Someone shares an item with you.
- A new member joins your organization.
- A story is published to you.
- A playbook run you started, or a scheduled run of your playbook, finishes.

Click a notification to open the linked item. Use the check icon to mark everything read, or the expand icon to open the full **Notifications** page, where you can switch between **All** and **Unread** and search your notifications.

Notifications are scoped to your active organization — if you work in more than one, switch organizations to see that organization's feed. Email notification preferences live under **Settings → Notifications**.
